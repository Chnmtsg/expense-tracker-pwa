# UI Guidelines

## Design Philosophy

Simple.

Clean.

Professional.

Financial applications should reduce cognitive load.

---

## Colors

Maintain consistent themes.

Avoid excessive colors.

High contrast.

---

## Typography

Readable.

Clear hierarchy.

Consistent sizing.

---

## Spacing

Use an 8px spacing system.

Avoid cramped layouts.

---

## Cards

Consistent padding.

Consistent border radius.

Consistent shadows.

---

## Dashboard

Most important information first.

Reduce visual clutter.

---

## Mobile

Design mobile-first.

Minimum touch target 44x44 px.

No horizontal scrolling.

---

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast.

Keyboard friendly.

Visible focus indicators.

# Expense Tracker Project

## Project Vision

Expense Tracker is a modern personal finance application.

The goal is NOT simply to record income and expenses.

The goal is to help users understand, manage and improve their financial life.

---

## Core Modules

- Dashboard
- Salary Calculator
- Income
- Expenses
- Budget Planning
- Analytics
- Reports
- Settings

---

## Project Principles

The application must be

- Fast
- Simple
- Offline-first
- Mobile-friendly
- Easy to understand
- Reliable

---

## Target Users

People with little accounting knowledge.

Every screen should be understandable without training.

---

## Long-term Vision

Future versions may include

- Cloud Sync
- AI Budget Assistant
- Debt Planner
- Savings Planner
- Investment Tracker
- OCR Receipt Scanner
- Notifications

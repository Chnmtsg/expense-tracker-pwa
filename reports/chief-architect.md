## The C36 Ruling — the reading, stated so it can be quoted

`C36` is **one fact per user-facing claim, from the source the app can defend** (`HANDOFF.md`, standing conventions). Round 16 restated its disclosure limb in the Architecture Strategy: *a figure the application computed by a model of its own is labelled as such wherever it is presented as a fact about the outside world.* UI Review is right that the reading was never settled. It is settled now.

**`C36` is a rule about co-visibility, not about existence. The test is: wherever the labelled figure can be read, its disclosure can be read without the user performing an action.** A control the user must operate fails that test, because the figure is legible before the gesture and the qualification is not — the application would be making its strongest claim in its loudest voice and its correction in a voice the user must go looking for. A `C36` disclosure may be moved, re-worded, shortened, or merged with another sentence. It may not be placed behind a `<details>`, a tooltip, a detail sheet, a second screen, or any other gesture, while the figure it qualifies is on screen.

Two consequences for this round, both verified at source in `renderDebts`' summary-block comment:

1. **The third sentence — *"It is spread evenly across your repayments, so it may not match your lender's own statement"* — is the `C36` disclosure for every "Cost so far" surface in the module, the tile and each per-card chip.** The file says so itself, in the one-site paragraph: `showCost` is the aggregate, so the single sentence is what qualifies every per-card chip rather than repeating once per debt. In the committed render, four of the five cards carry a "Cost so far" chip. Collapsing that sentence puts five unqualified statements of this application's own even-allocation arithmetic on screen as facts about five contracts it has never seen. **UI-01's primary shape is refused.**
2. **The second sentence — *"The figure above is the part of that extra you have paid so far, and it is not counted in your Net Balance"* — is not a model disclosure.** It states scope: what the figure is a part of, and where it does not appear. `C36` does not bind it, and collapsing it is permitted in principle. That is the fallback shape, and it survives `C36`.

**The fallback is nonetheless not built this round.** Its economics do not hold up under the evidence actually in hand, and the evidence in hand is weaker than the report treats it as being. See `WORK-03` in Deferred, and the risk recorded below about UI-01's viewport arithmetic.

## Conflict Rulings

### C-1 — Sequencing: values first, or instruments first

**Ruling: instruments first, affirmed — and the cost the Engineering Manager priced is refused along with it.** Code Review is right on the principle and the Engineering Manager is right to pay for it: the card's height is the one quantity in this module nothing has ever asserted, `t.F_pay_top_closed` records it and compares it to nothing, and without the flow the claim "one more whole card per screen" is a screenshot — which is how the card reached 294px across ten rulings. `C37` also requires the author of a condition to demonstrate red-then-green, and that demonstration is impossible if the value moves first.

**But the day of invisible work is not paid.** It was priced as two S items, and the larger of the two — `WORK-01`'s merge — is rejected above. What remains is one S flow, and it is authored first, run red against the unchanged application first, and **committed together with `WORK-04`**, which is its assertion partner by Code Review's own recommendation. So instruments come first in authorship and the owner sees a change in the first commit. The ordering is honoured in full and it costs nothing visible.

**On pulling `WORK-04` forward as the one early visible win: the concession is unnecessary and I am declining it as framed.** `WORK-04` is not pulled ahead of the instrument; it carries it. Shipping the one change whose entire purpose is to remove height, ahead of the only instrument that can see height, would be the eleventh ruling assessed exactly the way the first ten were.

### C-2 — Who decides the chip row's shape, and whether it gets fixed at all

**Ruling: Code Review is right that the shape is mine, and UI Review is right that "close it unfixed" is a legitimate outcome. I am choosing it. The chip row closes unfixed this round.** Full reasoning in the Rejected table under `WORK-07`; the short form is that the proposed shape turns a tag into a bar, leaves the height half of "ragged" untouched, and would be accepted or refused by eyeball. The complaint is carried in Deferred with a trigger and with an instrument that will, for the first time, have measured it. **Said out loud, as the Engineering Manager asked:** the owner's literal third complaint gets no direct fix this round. What it gets is the removal of the empty grey slab and the end of the seven-equal-blocks rhythm, which are the two causes of untidiness this project can state without taste, and a measurement of the rest.

### C-3 — Severity and effort on the literals

**Ruling: both severities stand exactly as filed, and the Engineering Manager handled this correctly — only the reviewer who raised a finding may set its severity, and priority is theirs.** The effort disagreement resolves by scope rather than by arbitration: the portion inside `WORK-06`'s own declarations is XS and rides that commit, as UI-06 argued; the standalone remainder is the S that Code Review priced, and I have rejected it as work rather than re-priced it. The reading I added at `WORK-08` in the Rejected table — *a block is opened by the declarations a commit edits* — is what stops this recurring as a standing obligation to sweep.

### The open ruling — `WORK-03` / UI-01 / `C36`

Ruled in full in **The C36 Ruling** above and in **Deferred**. In one line: **primary refused permanently on `C36`; fallback permitted by `C36`, deferred behind a measurement; neither is built this round.** UI Review did exactly what the constraint requires — named the ruling, argued against its stated reason, and flagged the tension rather than resolving it — and its argument against the *closure* is sound: a closure that caps growth does not require the capped content to be always-on. That argument wins on sentences 1 and 2 and loses on sentence 3, because sentence 3 is not capped content, it is the label that makes another figure honest.

### The `.debt-pct` risk the Engineering Manager flagged on `WORK-01`

**Ruled, and the risk is removed rather than managed.** The Engineering Manager's warning was that the merge must leave `.debt-pct`'s font-size as a deliberate debt-only declaration or `WORK-05` silently resizes the Savings Goals percentage, against round-16 `WORK-07`. With the merge rejected there is no shared selector for it to leak through, and round-16 `WORK-07` needs no special handling — it is simply still true. **The general invariant, binding on every commit in this round: no declaration written for the Debts card's density may live in a selector that also matches a `.goal-*` element.** It is checkable by reading the diff, which is the property I want, and the corrected comment under `WORK-01` is what tells the next reader the divergence was chosen.

## Architecture Strategy — next quarter

**What stays, and is not open for discussion.** Everything in the round-9 standing decision, the Round 11–16 supplementals and the ten design-request rulings, in full. The single self-contained `expense-pwa\index.html` that runs by being opened from disk: no framework, no build step, no bundler, no component abstraction. `localStorage`, one write seam, quarantine before write, numbered append-only migrations. **Offline-first, mobile-first, and correctness of financial data above everything** — this round applies that last clause to a *label* rather than an arithmetic, which is the second time in three rounds the honesty of this module turned on wording rather than on a formula. `C36`, `C38`, `C39`, `C40`, `C42`, `ARCH-01`, `C43` unchanged. Every figure, derivation, gate, rate sentence, copy closure and storage decision in the Debts module is untouched by this ruling.

**What changes.**

1. **The debt card and the goal card stop being twins, and the comment says so.** Round 16 made `.debt-pct` a per-module rule; this round generalises that to the card's whole vertical rhythm. A rule is shared between these two modules only where the two share a *meaning* — `.goal-bar` is a length, `.goal-meta-item` is a tag, `button.goal-add` is a 44px target. Density is not a shared meaning, and a comment asserting an identity that the next density change will end is worse than two rules, because it stops the next reader checking.
2. **The Debts screen acquires its first geometry guard, and it guards a product statement rather than a number:** at least one complete debt card is reachable without scrolling. That sentence is the guarantee; the pixels are its implementation. Every other geometry figure the flow gathers is labelled diagnostic in the output, per `C42`, so nothing that compares against nothing can be mistaken for a guard.
3. **A block is "opened" by the declarations a commit edits**, not by the file, module or rule-set they live in. This bounds the standing token-conversion convention so it can never become the app-wide sweep that stays rejected.
4. **`C36` is a co-visibility rule**, stated at the top of this ruling in the form it should be quoted in. A model disclosure may be moved, shortened or merged; it may not be put behind a gesture while the figure it qualifies is on screen.

**Off limits this quarter — everything on the prior lists, plus four:**

- **Collapsing, tooltipping, sheet-hiding or otherwise gating any `C36` disclosure behind a user action, anywhere in the application.** This is the round's main addition and it is general, not Debts-specific.
- **A second disclosure control on the Debts screen.** The screen has one `<details>`, for the add form, ruled in round 16. A second one on the same screen teaches the user that this screen hides things, which is the opposite of what the first one was for. If `WORK-03`'s fallback ever fires it reuses that same primitive and adds no other.
- **Any `.goal-*` selector inside a Debts density commit**, and any Debts density declaration in a selector that also matches a `.goal-*` element.
- **A geometry assertion written as a literal pixel count.** The relationship or nothing. This project has had four wrong derived pixel figures and a width-mode probe that silently reported the wrong width; a height literal is the next member of that family.

**Risks I am recording, not scheduling. None is a finding and no reviewer raised any of them as one.**
